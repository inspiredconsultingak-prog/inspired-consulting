How We Got Here

A Decade of Real-World Experience

Inspired Consulting was founded by Alicia and Ryan Betzler — two entrepreneurs who have collectively owned and operated over five successful businesses across their careers, most recently a restaurant and delivery business.

After a decade of consulting with small business owners, we kept seeing the same problem: great business owners being stretched impossibly thin across too many vendors, too many systems, and too many moving parts. We experienced it ourselves.

We built Inspired Consulting to be the solution we wished we had — a single, trusted partner that could handle the full picture of running and growing a business, without the overhead of managing five different relationships to do it.
